App Title: MyBookCatalog
Short Description: Explore a curated collection of literary masterpieces and modern hits.
Full Description:
MyBookCatalog is your digital companion for exploring a wide range of books across various genres. From historical thrillers and science fiction to self-help and wellness, our catalog provides detailed insights into every title.

Key Features:
- Comprehensive list of trending books.
- Detailed information: View author, genre, and plot summaries.
- Clean and intuitive UI for easy navigation.
- Integrated mobile money simulation for book purchases.
